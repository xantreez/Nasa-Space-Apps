`weather` swaps screen overlays with 1s fades: calm = plain sky; flare = orange rim light + lens flare + sun glow; storm = red tint, pulsing vignette, diagonal radiation streaks; night = Sun hidden, blue wash, lit windows/screens, frost sparkles, brighter Earth. `built` adds pop-in structures: o2-generator/garden/big-dish/storm-shelter/battery-bank/science-lab grow the matching location, colony adds domes + tubes, city adds skyline, rover and flag.
`at` places Tobish at a location; setting `target` makes him hop there (1-1.8 s, faces travel direction, dust puffs) and then emits `arrived(target)` (parent should reset `target` to null afterwards); clicking/Enter on a location emits `go(loc)` unless `disabled`; `highlight` = pulsing gold ring, gold pill and bouncing arrow.
Layout switches by container aspect (portrait = 600x1000 composition, landscape = 1600x900); `day` slides the Sun slowly across the sky; all motion respects prefers-reduced-motion.
